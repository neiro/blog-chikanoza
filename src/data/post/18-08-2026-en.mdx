## A scheduled day of resuscitation

I built nothing new today.

I spent the whole day keeping a small server on the operating table: bringing it back up, persuading it not to fall over, and dealing with that database. MS SQL occasionally showed signs of life, apparently only to make its next collapse more dramatic.

Everything should already work. Technically, it sometimes even does. Never for long, though. Probably to keep everyone’s expectations at a medically safe level.

And why is that? Because the DBA is now in year two of “learning,” gaining experience, and getting up to speed. It is a remarkably humane onboarding programme: nobody disturbs the person, knowledge is never rushed, and responsibility maintains a respectful distance. I would love to work like that: you keep doing something, while results politely avoid interrupting the process.

While the DBA gets up to speed, the server slips into a coma. I reach the evening knowing that I spent the entire day raising something that should have been standing on its own a long time ago.

We will continue monitoring the patient tomorrow. It may even survive the night.
