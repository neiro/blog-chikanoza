The contact form opens your email client and sends the message directly to `viacheslav.redkin@chikanoza.com`. A message may contain your name, email address, and any information you choose to include. This information is used only to reply to you.

Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through the form.

Hosting and content-delivery providers may process standard technical data such as an IP address, request time, browser information, and requested URL to deliver and protect the website. This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics at this time.

The website contains links to external services such as Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, and RSS readers. Their own privacy policies apply when you leave this website.

## Retention and requests

Contact messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request and maintain a useful correspondence. To ask about, correct, or delete a message you sent, email `viacheslav.redkin@chikanoza.com`.
